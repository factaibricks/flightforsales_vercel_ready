Cancellation & Refund Policy

At Parallel Flights, cancellation and refund requests are handled according to the fare rules and conditions of the airline associated with your booking.

Cancellation Policy

If you need to cancel or modify your reservation, please contact our customer support team as early as possible. Eligibility for cancellation depends on the airline, fare type, and ticket conditions.

Refund Policy

  • Refund eligibility is determined by the airline's fare rules.
  • Processing time varies depending on the airline and payment method.
  • Airline cancellation charges and applicable service fees may apply.
  • Promotional or non-refundable tickets may not qualify for refunds.

Changes to Bookings

Flight date changes, name corrections, or itinerary modifications are subject to airline policies and seat availability. Additional fare differences may apply.
