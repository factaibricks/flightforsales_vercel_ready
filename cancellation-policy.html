Cancellation & Refund Policy

At Parallel Flights, cancellations and refunds are processed according to the fare rules and policies of the airline associated with your booking.

Cancellation

If you need to cancel or modify your reservation, please contact our customer support team as early as possible. Cancellation eligibility depends on the airline and ticket type purchased.

Refund Eligibility

  • Refunds are subject to the airline's fare rules.
  • Processing times vary depending on the airline and payment method.
  • Applicable airline cancellation charges and service fees may apply.
  • Some promotional or non-refundable tickets may not qualify for a refund.
